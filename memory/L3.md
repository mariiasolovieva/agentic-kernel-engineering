Source: `data/papers/shuaib2024_multikernel_amorphous.pdf`

The paper presents an automated active-learning workflow that clusters amorphous configurations using a hybrid local-plus-long-range average similarity kernel, samples a minimal training database from the clusters, and fits Gaussian approximation potentials whose hyperparameters are tuned by Bayesian optimization, yielding accurate and stable models that match first-principles and experimental data.
